# Facilitator run-of-show (90 min)

Goal: a **Cisco-wide** story. Aaron opens. Kapil owns the science. Ivan owns most live clicking. Peter frames Cisco and the bundle — he does not narrate every pane.

## Cast

- **Aaron Byers** — Elastic AE — room, intros, close the asks
- **Peter Simkins** — Elastic SA — problem + bundle only (plus a sentence per team while Ivan clicks)
- **Kapil Jadhav** — guest — embeddings, multimodal, 5 min split-pane demo
- **Ivan Silva** — Elastic — CRM / Lifecycle / Webex demos
- Named Cisco owners in the story: **Anshul (CRM Analytics)**, **Balaji (Lifecycle Platform)**, Webex / Infra

## Beat sheet

1. **0–5 Aaron** — `/slides?section=opening`. Name the four voices. Promise: not a one-person show.
2. **5–10 Peter** — `/slides?section=cisco`. Pipes vs understanding. Gartner 4–8h / 4–7h. Three teams.
3. **10–25 Kapil** — `/slides?section=tech` then `/demo`. Three queries: lock-in, switching costs, legal hold. Then Discover on `cisco-jina-corpus`.
4. **20–30 Ivan (+ Peter one-liners)** — `/crm` (Anshul), `/lifecycle` (Balaji), `/webex` (Gov East/West).
5. **30–35 Peter** — `/bundle`. Elastic runs search; Jina powers relevance.
6. **35–90** — learners on Instruqt. Speakers roam in their lanes. Aaron collects the three asks.

## Anti-patterns

- Peter answering Kapil’s embedding questions.
- Skipping Umbrella “legal hold” (that is the false-positive teaching moment).
- “We’ll follow up” with no named Cisco owner.
